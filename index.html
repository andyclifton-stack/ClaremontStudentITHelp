Quick reminders

A few habits prevent most laptop and account problems: keep your device charged, restart regularly, and save school work where it is backed up.

  • Charge your laptop before school
  • Restart when updates need to finish
  • Keep OneDrive signed in and running
  • Speak to your form tutor if you are stuck
